About Us

Welcome to Floreal, where creativity and craftsmanship come together to bring you a delightful array of handcrafted crochet items. Our passion for crochet is woven into every piece we create, from delicate flowers to cozy cardigans, charming key chains and beautiful pots.

Our journey began with a simple love for crochet and over time, it has blossomed into a diverse collection of handmade treasures. Each item in our shop is meticulously crafted with attention to detail and a commitment to quality, ensuring that you receive nothing but the best.

Based in India, we are proud to offer our unique creations to customers around the globe. Our collection features a wide range of products. Each product is a testament to our dedication to craftsmanship. Our team of skilled artisans brings their expertise and creativity to every piece, ensuring that each item is not only beautiful but also durable and long-lasting.

Whether you're looking to add a touch of elegance to your home with our decor items, searching for the perfect gift or planning a special event, our diverse range of crochet products offers something for every occasion.

At Floreal, we believe in the power of handmade creations. We are dedicated to providing our customers with products that are not only beautiful but also sustainable and eco-friendly. Our crochet items are perfect for those who appreciate the charm and uniqueness of handmade goods. Thank you for visiting our website and for supporting our small business. We look forward to bringing a bit of crochet magic into your life.
